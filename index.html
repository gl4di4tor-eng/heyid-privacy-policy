Privacy Policy – HEYID

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

HEYID is a chat application that allows users to communicate with people around the world. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following data:

  • Email address (for registration and login)
  • Profile information (name, age, city, country, bio)
  • Chat messages sent through the app
  • Device information for app functionality
  • Push notification token (to deliver notifications about new messages)
  • Advertising ID and other device and usage data collected by Google AdMob to show ads (see section 5)

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Provide and improve the HEYID service
  • Enable communication between users
  • Send you notifications about new messages
  • Ensure the security of the platform
  • Show advertising in the app, which helps keep HEYID available

4. Data Storage and Service Providers

Your account data and messages are stored using Supabase cloud infrastructure. Push notifications are delivered using Firebase Cloud Messaging provided by Google. We do not sell your account data, profile information or chat messages to third parties.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

HEYID displays ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and use certain data from your device, including:

  • Your Android advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (such as device model, operating system version, language)
  • Information about how you interact with ads

This data is used to deliver, measure and, where you have given consent, personalize ads. We do not share the content of your chat messages or your profile details with advertisers.

Google and its partners act as independent controllers of the data they collect for advertising. You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's Privacy Policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Your Advertising Choices and Consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalized, using Google's consent management platform. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at the address below.
  • On your Android device you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path may differ depending on your device).
  • If you opt out of personalized ads, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

7. Age Requirement

HEYID is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or want to exercise your rights, please contact us at: heyid.app@gmail.com

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes.
